Definition
----------
A subsequence of a list or string is a contiguous or non-contiguous sequence that follows an order.

array - [3, 1, 2]
subsequences are:
[3]
[1]
[2]
[3, 1]
[3, 2]
[1, 2]
[3, 1, 2]

*** Number of subsequence = 2 ^ n 
where n is the size.

*** Number of sub-array = n * (n + 1) / 2;
